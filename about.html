About

Discover your story within

Understory Tarot is a place to read the cards as stories, and to find, in them, what you already hold.

In a forest, the understory is the layer of growth that happens beneath the canopy. It is the growth on the forest floor that happens in low light and imperfect conditions.

Tarot is a story telling tool. The cards give us images and symbols that tell us a story. We can hold that up against our life, and see what it has to tell us. My role is to help you find what applies to you in the story and make meaning from it.

Many of my clients come to me after leaving the high-control religion they grew up in. Some of them are looking for a spirituality that is their own to create. Some are looking for clarity within themselves, and utilize the reading to help them find it. You are welcome here whatever shape your question takes.
